🛡️ Quantitative Validation Reference

Technical Confluence Gates

  • Trend Strength (ADX ≥ 25.0): Calculated using Wilder's RMA. Ensures the stock is in a definitive directional trend, filtering out horizontal chop and consolidation drift.
  • Institutional Footprint (RVOL ≥ 1.0x): Relative volume versus the 20-day volume SMA. Confirms that professional money is defending the pullback.
  • Rising Support (50 SMA Slope > 0%): Evaluates the 5-day percentage change of the 50-day moving average so slope is price-scale invariant across the TSX universe.
  • Market Outperformance (RS vs XIU.TO > 0%): 63-day Rate of Change comparison against the TSX 60 benchmark, ensuring capital flows only into market leaders.
  • Entry Location (Pullback ≤ 2.5% above 50 SMA): Prevents chasing extended breakouts and avoids breakdowns. Long entries require the close to hold at or above the 50 SMA within 2.5%.
  • Candle Confirmation (CLV ≥ 0.60): Requires a bullish close finishing in the upper 60% of the day's range (Close Location Value), verifying active buyer defense.

Fundamental & Risk Safety Gates

  • Liquidity Gate (MDDV ≥ $5M CAD, Price ≥ $5): 20-day median of close × volume must be at least $5M CAD, and last close must be at least $5, so orders do not walk illiquid books or penny names.
  • Debt Service Coverage (≥ 2.0x): Operating income (EBITDA) must cover interest obligations by at least 2.0x. Financial institutions (banks/insurers) automatically bypass this metric.
  • Free Cash Flow (FCF > 0): Trailing Twelve Month (TTM) cash flow must be positive to ensure business solvency without continuous capital dilution.
  • Earnings Blackout (-2 to +7 Days): Hard blocks setups during reporting weeks to eliminate gap risk.
  • Headline Velocity (7-Day NLP Filter): Scans news for litigation, short-seller alerts, regulatory probes, and sudden management resignations.
  • Macro Sentiment & Volatility (VIX Sizing): Blocks long positions during Extreme Greed (>75) and scales position size inversely with the CBOE VIX index.
